# Editorial policy

User instruction, 26 October 2026:

- Preserve the original author's text, statements, formulas and arguments in the body, including errors in the original printing.
- Put editorial corrections, qualifications, counterexamples and supplied proof steps in clearly identified footnotes.
- If the editorial discussion would exceed one typeset page, use a separate, clearly labelled editorial remark section, with a short footnote directing the reader to it.
- Correct OCR/transcription errors against the scan; an OCR rewrite is not original text to preserve. Doubao text is not authoritative.
- Continue the agreed notation modernization and linked references. These changes must preserve the mathematical distinctions in the original.
- Distinguish the author's printed errata from new editorial corrections. Review prior inline mathematical corrections against this policy rather than assuming Git HEAD is a faithful transcription of the scan.
- Use Git history to recover previous wording, and verify it against the scan where its provenance is uncertain. Do not revert unrelated work or discard a correction when moving it into a note.

Current conversion evidence and remaining historical checks are recorded in audit/PROGRESS.md. The restoration pass of 26 October covers the recent uncommitted mathematical edits and selected earlier documented original errors; it is not a claim that every earlier inline change has been reclassified.
